# Open legal questions

For the founder and a lawyer. Agents add questions here instead of assuming. Mark answers with the date and source.

1. **Operator identity:** legal entity, address, CUI, contact and DPO (if any) for the privacy policy, terms and
   imprint.
2. **Omnibus:** we show, per offer, the lowest price of the last 30 days from our own history. Is it allowed to
   show the feed's "old price" / discount percentage before we have 30 days of history for that offer, or should we
   hide reductions until then? Who is responsible for the reference price: the shop or us as a comparator?
3. **Product images and descriptions:** may we store, resize and serve feed images (and texts) from our own storage
   under the 2Performant and Profitshare terms and the shops' terms?
4. **P2B (Reg. 2019/1150) and consumer law:** wording of the public "How we rank" page; required disclosure of
   affiliate remuneration and of promoted positions on listings (not only on the product page).
5. **Affiliate network terms:** any restrictions on price comparison use, link cloaking via `/go/`, brand bidding,
   or showing the same shop's offers from two networks.
6. **DSA:** are we an "online platform" for merchant reviews? Required notice & action flow, statement of reasons,
   internal complaint handling, contact point, transparency reporting thresholds.
7. **Reviews:** verification of "real customer" claims (Omnibus rules on consumer reviews): we cannot verify
   purchases; how to disclose this.
8. **Retention:** proposed 90 days for view history, raw clicks aggregated daily and deleted after 30 days,
   price history kept indefinitely (no personal data). OK?
9. **Cookies/consent:** only essential cookies (session, locale), no third-party tracking. Is a banner still needed,
   or only an information page?
10. **Price alert emails/push:** consent wording and unsubscribe requirements (ePrivacy / Romanian Law 506/2004).
    Current state (Phase 4): the email links to `/cont/alerte` (sign-in required) plus a `List-Unsubscribe` header to
    the same page; is a no-login one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) required for these transactional alerts?
11. **DSA notices without an account:** review reports currently require sign-in. Must notice & action (Art. 16) be
    open to anyone (e.g. a form with an email address)?
